# Web Novel Writing

## Workflow

1. Keep the author's requested scope: topic, outline, chapter, review, and prose are different deliverables. Do not draft prose unless asked.
2. Before outlining, identify the reader-facing promise and repeatable story engine. Make the opening demonstrate both rather than explain them.
3. Before drafting, recover only the canon, relationships, and unresolved promises the scene needs.
4. Plan each scene around a character's immediate want, resistance, leverage, choice, and changed situation. Let world rules enter through that action.
5. Give important voices a distinguishable goal and conversational tactic. For flirtation, let mutual attraction coexist with agency, boundaries, and stakes; do not substitute repeated looks or bodily reactions for relationship change.
6. After drafting, check causal continuity, character knowledge and goals, relationship movement, chapter-title integrity, and whether the ending earns its forward pull.

## Platform Fit

Tune pacing, chapter shape, and prose density to the author's brief and target platform. Treat quantitative conventions as signals, not universal quotas. Do not force a cliffhanger, tragedy, or specific relationship shape when the story does not call for it.

## Project Integration

The automatic pipeline uses compact stage contracts in core/webnovel_craft.py for chapter planning, plot expansion, drafting, and evidence-backed continuity review. Keep durable facts in project canon and memory; this skill complements project state, title checks, the factual critic, and the language-only humanizer.

Approach adapted from [oh-story-claudecode](https://github.com/zenstory-ai/oh-story-claudecode) (opening promise and pacing), [Chinese-WebNovel-Skill](https://github.com/Tomsawyerhu/Chinese-WebNovel-Skill/tree/v2) (dialogue and endings), and [Novel Control Station](https://github.com/jingtai123/Novel-Control-Station-Skill) (chapter preparation and recall). The engine does not execute upstream scripts or use external book corpora.
